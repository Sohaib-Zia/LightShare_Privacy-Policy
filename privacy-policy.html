Light Share privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Who we are

Light Share ("the app") is made by Red Light ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses and what it does not use. If you have a question, write to redlight.privacy.support@gmail.com.

In short

  • We do not have servers for your files. Your files, photos, videos, messages and transfer history are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • The app has no account, no sign-up and no analytics or crash-reporting service.
  • The Google Play version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may use your device's advertising ID and some device information to show and measure ads (see "Advertising" below).
  • Files travel directly from one device to another on the same Wi-Fi network or hotspot, only after you approve the other device.

What the app does

Light Share sends files, folders, apps and text messages between devices that are on the same Wi-Fi network or hotspot. The other person can use the Light Share app, or just open a web page in any browser. Transfers do not go through the internet and do not use any server of ours.

Information we collect

We do not collect, receive or store personal information about you. We cannot see who you are, what you share, who you share with, or where you are.

Information stored on your device only

  • Settings (for example language, theme, device name and the options you switch on or off).
  • Transfer history: file names, sizes, times, direction (sent or received) and the other device's name or network address.
  • For phone-to-phone sharing: a security certificate made on your phone, and the name and fingerprint of devices you chose to pair with.
  • Text messages you type in the chat of a share exist only while the share is running and are not saved afterwards.

This information stays on your device. You can clear the history and remove paired devices in the app, and uninstalling the app removes everything.

Permissions and why the app asks for them

  • Network, Wi-Fi state and multicast: to share files and to find nearby devices. The internet permission is also used to show ads in the Google Play version.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob to show ads. You can reset or delete it in Android Settings.
  • Camera: only on the Scan QR screen, to read a QR code. Pictures are not recorded, saved or sent.
  • Nearby Wi-Fi devices (Android 13 and newer) or Location (Android 12 and older): only when you start a hotspot or look for nearby devices, because Android requires it. The app does not read or store your location.
  • Notifications and foreground service: to show a running transfer and let you stop it, and to keep a transfer going while you use other apps.
  • Files, photos and folders: the app reads only what you choose or share to it. Received files are saved in the folder you choose, or in Downloads/Light Share.
  • Installed apps list: used only on the Apps screen, on your device, so you can choose an app to send. It is not sent anywhere.

Sharing with other devices and encryption

Everything you send goes only to the device you chose and approved. Please note:

  • Sharing with a web browser uses plain HTTP and is not encrypted. Anyone on the same Wi-Fi network could in theory see it, so use it on networks you trust. You approve each device, and you can turn on a PIN.
  • Sharing between two phones that have the app uses "Fast share" by default, which is not encrypted either. You can turn Fast share off in Settings to use an encrypted (HTTPS) connection that checks the other phone's identity.
  • Do not use the app on public or shared Wi-Fi for private files unless Fast share is off, or use a hotspot of your own.

Advertising

The Google Play version of the app shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google's own privacy policy applies to this data: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app asks for your consent before showing ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy & security > Ad choices when it is shown, and you can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalisation in Android Settings > Google > Ads.

Ads never receive your files, file names, messages or transfer history. The version of the app that is not on Google Play (direct download) does not contain ads.

Third parties

Apart from Google AdMob (ads) and the Google Play services that Android and Google Play provide, the app does not send information to any third party and has no other third-party tracking or analytics.

Retention and deletion

We do not hold your information, so there is nothing for us to delete. Information on your device can be deleted by you: clear the history and remove paired devices in the app, delete received files in your file manager, or uninstall the app. To delete the advertising ID or ad data held by Google, use Android Settings > Google > Ads and your Google Account controls.

Security

Files are sent only to devices you approve, and each share link works for a short time and for the approved device only. No method of sending data is perfectly safe; see the encryption notes above and choose the options that suit how private your files are.

Children

Light Share is not directed at children under 13 and is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

Because we do not collect your personal information, we cannot look up, correct or delete it. If you live where laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA apply, you may still contact us with any request or question and we will answer. For ad data, you can use Google's controls described above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be shown in the app and on this page with a new date.
